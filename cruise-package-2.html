Ultimate Cruise Escape

Discover the culture, nature and authenticity of Roatán with our Ultimate Cruise Escape. This package takes you beyond the typical tourist experience to explore the real island.

📍 Pickup & drop-off directly at the cruise port

Starting from $115 p.p · Minimum 4 people

Includes a special surprise gift from Roa Tours & Co. as a thank-you for choosing us 🎁✨

What's Included

🚢

Pick up directly from the cruise port for a smooth, comfortable, and worry-free island experience from the moment you arrive in Roatán

🌴

Enjoy a scenic stop at one of Roatán's beautiful viewpoints, perfect for breathtaking island photos and panoramic ocean views

🏝️

Experience a cultural drive through the local communities of French Harbour, where guests can see authentic island life, colorful neighborhoods, and stop for memorable photos along the way

🦥

Visit the beautiful Hacienda del Cielo animal park, where guests can enjoy monkeys, sloths, peacocks, ducks, rabbits, ponies, goats, donkeys, and more in a relaxing tropical environment

🍦

Inside the park, guests can also enjoy optional extras including an ice cream stop, coffee shop, refreshing drinks and natural juices, a mini chocolate experience, souvenir shop, picnic areas, and a playground area for children

🌊

Continue the journey toward Santos Guardiola while enjoying beautiful coastal scenery, island viewpoints, and photo opportunities along the way

🚤

Enjoy a relaxing mangrove boat tour through Roatán's peaceful natural waterways, surrounded by stunning tropical landscapes and island wildlife

🍽️

Stop for lunch in the charming area of Jonesville, where guests can optionally purchase delicious local food and enjoy the authentic island atmosphere

🎶

Drive through the vibrant Garifuna community of Punta Gorda, known for its rich culture, colorful surroundings, and island history

❄️

Comfortable air-conditioned transportation included throughout the entire tour

⏰

Safe and reliable transportation back to the cruise port with enough time before your ship departs, always taking traffic and delays into consideration

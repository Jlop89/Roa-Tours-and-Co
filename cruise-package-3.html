Caribbean Tour

Enjoy the best of Roatán's marine life and island culture with our Caribbean Tour. Snorkel in crystal-clear waters, explore local flavors, and relax on beautiful West Bay Beach.

📍 Pickup & drop-off directly at the cruise port

Starting from $135 p.p · Minimum 4 people

Includes a special surprise gift from Roa Tours & Co. as a thank-you for choosing us 🎁✨

What's Included

🚢

Pick up directly from the cruise port for a smooth and relaxing island adventure from the moment you arrive in Roatán

🌴

Enjoy a scenic stop at one of Roatán's breathtaking viewpoints, perfect for unforgettable island photos and panoramic ocean views

🌺

Visit the iconic Roatán sign for the perfect tropical photo opportunity and vacation memories

🌊

Explore the colorful and vibrant area of West End, where guests can enjoy the island atmosphere, local culture, and beautiful coastal scenery

🍫

Stop at the famous chocolate factory and discover delicious local chocolate flavors made right here on the island

🚤

Continue to beautiful West Bay Beach, where guests will board a boat for an unforgettable snorkeling combo experience

🐠

The snorkeling combo includes 2 snorkeling stops with approximately 1 hour in the water at each location, guided by a professional divemaster who will provide a safety briefing, assistance during the activity, and share interesting information about Roatán's incredible marine life, coral reefs, and tropical fish

🤿

All snorkeling equipment is included for the activity, including masks, fins, and life jackets, which are mandatory at all times for both children and adults

🐬

During the boat experience, guests will also enjoy a relaxing ride through the mangrove lagoon and pass by the dolphin sanctuary area for outside photo opportunities

🍍

Fresh fruit and bottled water will be provided between snorkeling stops for a refreshing tropical experience

🏖️

After the snorkeling adventure, guests will return to West Bay Beach to relax and enjoy beach time in paradise

🍹

Includes 1 complimentary beach chair per person at the beautiful Henry Morgan Resort beach facilities, where guests can also optionally purchase a delicious beachfront lunch and refreshing drinks

❄️

Comfortable air-conditioned transportation included throughout the entire tour

⏰

Safe and reliable transportation back to the cruise port with enough time before your ship departs, always taking traffic and delays into consideration

Cruise Ship Adventure Package

Experience the best of Roatán with our all-inclusive Cruise Ship Adventure Package. From thrilling activities to relaxing beach time, this package is designed to give you a complete island experience during your cruise stop.

📍 Pickup & drop-off directly at the cruise port

Starting from $80 p.p · Minimum 4 people

Includes a special surprise gift from Roa Tours & Co. as a thank-you for choosing us 🎁✨

What's Included

🚢

Pick up directly from the cruise port for a comfortable and stress-free experience from the moment you arrive in Roatán

🌴

Enjoy a scenic stop at one of the island's beautiful viewpoints, perfect for unforgettable photos and breathtaking ocean views

🦥

Adventure at the canopy and animal park, where guests can enjoy exciting activities, interact with amazing island wildlife, or choose to visit only the animal park experience

🛍️

Visit local souvenir shops to find handmade gifts, island treasures, and memorable keepsakes from Roatán

🍫

Stop at the famous chocolate factory and discover delicious local chocolate flavors made right here on the island

🌊

Explore the vibrant area of West End, where guests may choose to walk around, shop, or simply enjoy a panoramic drive through this colorful beach town

🍰

Visit the well-known Rum Cake factory for a sweet island treat and local flavors

🏖️

Relax and enjoy beach time in West Bay with 1 complimentary beach chair per person at the beautiful Henry Morgan Resort beach facilities, where guests can also optionally purchase a delicious beachfront lunch and refreshing drinks

❄️

Comfortable air-conditioned transportation included throughout the entire tour

⏰

Safe and reliable transportation back to the cruise port with enough time before your ship departs, always taking traffic and delays into consideration

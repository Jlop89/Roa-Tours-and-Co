Dolphin Encounter

$100 per person (minimum 2 people)

Duration: 30 minutes
Ages: 3+

Meet a dolphin face-to-face in this gentle, waist-deep introduction. Wade into the clear Caribbean waters and enjoy a close interaction with one of our friendly Atlantic Bottlenose Dolphins. You'll learn about dolphin behavior from our expert trainers while sharing a kiss, a handshake, and a moment you'll never forget.

Perfect for: First-time dolphin visitors, families with young children, and anyone seeking a relaxed and educational encounter.

$155 per person (minimum 2 people)
